About the space

A 1,200 sq ft loft with a skylight that brings beautiful light all day long.

Furnished with an ivory curved sofa, black leather sofa, accent chairs, grey sofa, coffee table, decorative lamp, high chairs and paper backdrops. Photography equipment and grip included, plus a kitchen, restrooms and dressing room.

Ideal for lookbooks, portraits, model tests, family shoots, video and events. More on Instagram @soulstudio.us.
